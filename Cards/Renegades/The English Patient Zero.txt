The English Patient Zero

- Unveil {2}  
- When the English Patient Zero is unveiled, each opponent discards a card, loses 3 life, sacrifices an artifact, a creature, an enchantment, a land, a planeswalker and a battle, then exiles 5 cards from their graveyard, mills 3, then removes 3 counters from among permanents they control, then gets a poison counter.  
- At the beginning of your upkeep, you may put a \-1/-1 counter on each of up to 3 creatures.
