<TITLE>The Party Rock Anthem</TITLE>
<ROLE>Renegade</ROLE>
<RULES>
When The Party Rock Anthem is unveiled, you may say “Everyday I’m shuffling”.   
If you do shuffle your library. Then, switch this role card with a random Renegade role card from outside the game and turn all non-king role cards in play face down. Shuffle and redistribute them.  
Then shuffle your library.
</RULES>
