<TITLE>The GOAT</TITLE>
<ROLE>Renegade</ROLE>
<RULES>
When The GOAT is unveiled, until the end of your next turn all creatures you don't control are goaded and gain haste.  
- At the beginning of each opponent's upkeep, choose a creature that player controls. It becomes goaded.  
- Whenever a goaded creature deals damage to one of your opponents, draw a card.
- {flavor}"Greathearted, sure. Goat-headed? absolutely."
</RULES>
