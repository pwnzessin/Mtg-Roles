Behold the Beheld Beholder 

- When Behold the Beheld Beholder is unveiled, create 4 and at the beginning of your upkeep create 1 0/1 Eye creature tokens named “Eyestalk” with defender and shroud.   
- Whenever an Eyestalk you control dies or is exiled, choose a player. Reveal cards from the top of that player’s library until you reveal an instant or sorcery card with mana value less than or equal to the number of Eye creatures you control. You may cast that card without paying its mana cost. Put the other cards revealed this way on the bottom of that player’s library in a random order.  
- At the beginning of your end step, if you control more Eye creatures than the players left in the game have eyes, you win the game.
