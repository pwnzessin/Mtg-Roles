<TITLE>The Truther or Darer</TITLE>
<ROLE>Renegade</ROLE>
<SETCODE>2xm m</SETCODE>
<RULES>
Unveil only after your third turn and only as a sorcery.  
When The Truther or Darer is unveiled, and at the beginning of your upkeep, choose an opponent. That player chooses truth or dare {-}  
- • {indent} Truth {-} That player unveils their role card. If they can't pay its unveil cost, its unveil cost is {0} this turn.  
- • {indent} Dare {-} That player mills cards until only ten cards remain in their library.
