<TITLE>The Illusionist</TITLE>
<ROLE>Renegade</ROLE>
<SETCODE>2xm m</SETCODE>
<RULES>
Unveil only during your turn.  
At the beginning of your first main phase, choose another face-up role card that hasn’t been chosen this way before. The Illusionist becomes a copy of that role card. You may activate the unveil-ability of that role card. If that role card has an upkeep trigger, it triggers after The Illusionist becomes a copy of that role card.  
The Illusionist still only counts as a Renegade at all times.  
{flavor}"Man's reach exceeds his imagination." {-} Robert Angier
