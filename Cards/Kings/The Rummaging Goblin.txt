<TITLE>The Rummaging Goblin</TITLE>
<ROLE>King</ROLE>
<SETCODE>v11 r</SETCODE>
<ART_YPOS>0.07</ART_YPOS>
<RULES>
At the beginning of your upkeep, discard a card, then draw a card.  
Whenever you discard a nonland card this way, create a Treasure token.  
*To a goblin, value is based on the four S’s: shiny, stabby, smelly, and super smelly.*
</RULES>
