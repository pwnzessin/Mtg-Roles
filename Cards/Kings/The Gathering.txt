<RULES>
At the beginning of your upkeep, you may choose one that hasn’t been chosen -   
- White {-} You gain 8 life.  
- Blue {-} Draw two cards.  
- Black {-} Choose and destroy a creature or planeswalker.  
- Red {-} Create three 1/1 red Goblin creature tokens.  
- Green {-} Choose and destroy an artifact, enchantment or battle.  
- Multicolor {-} Gain 1 life, scry 1, deal 1 damage to any target, create a 1/1 red Goblin creature token, at the beginning of your precombat main phase gain 1 mana of any color.  
- Colorless {-} Create a “waste” basic-land token. It enters tapped with a stun counter on it. 
</RULES>
