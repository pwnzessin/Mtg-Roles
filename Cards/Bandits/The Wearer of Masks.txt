<TITLE>The Wearer of Masks</TITLE>
<ROLE>Bandit</ROLE>
<SETCODE>ktk m</SETCODE>
<RULES>
- When The Wearer of Masks is unveiled, choose four Bandit role cards at random from outside the game.  
- You may pay the unveil costs of one of those cards. The Wearer of Masks becomes a copy of that role card. (*Role cards without an unveil cost have unveil {0}.)*  
- If you don’t, turn The Wearer of Masks face down at the beginning of your next end step.  
</RULES>
