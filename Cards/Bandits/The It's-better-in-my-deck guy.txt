<TITLE>The It's-better-in-my-deck guy</TITLE>
<ROLE>Bandit</ROLE>
<SETCODE>ktk m</SETCODE>
<ART_YPOS>0.02</ART_YPOS>
<RULES>
When The it’s-better-in-my-deck-guy is unveiled, for each player other than you, choose another player at random, who has not been chosen already.  
That player gains control of that players commander.  
If that commander is in the command zone, that player may cast that commander by only paying the commander tax on it.  
At the beginning of your next turn's end step, return all commanders you don't control to their command zones.
</RULES>
