<TITLE>The Commander Support</TITLE>
<ROLE>Bandit</ROLE>
<SETCODE>ktk m</SETCODE>
<RULES>
Unveil only during your turn and only if a non-king, non-bandit role card is face up.  
When The Commander Support is unveiled, go to the kitchen and get everyone who wants a new drink.   
Then choose two emblems with {-}  
• {indent}“Your commander tax is {0}.”  
• {indent}“The first spell you cast each turn costs {1} less for each time you’ve cast your commander from the command zone this game.”  
• {indent}“Creatures you control get \+1/+1 for each time you’ve cast your commander from the command zone this game.”  
• {indent}“Commander creatures you own have base power and toughness 10/10.”  
Each Bandit gets one of the emblems chosen this way.
</RULES>
