<TITLE>The Scrapper</TITLE>
<ROLE>Bandit</ROLE>
<RULES>
When The Scrapper is unveiled, Choose five. You may choose the same mode more than once per player.
• {indent}BLAST: Create a tapped 5/1 colorless Gyro artifact creature token with flying and haste under chosen player's control. It has “When this creature dies, it deals 5 damage to you and 1 damage to each creature you control” and “Sacrifice this creature at the beginning of your next end step.” Goad it.  
• {indent}BULWARK: Create a 0/5 Gyro artifact creature token with flying, defender under chosen player's control. It has “When this creature enters, you gain hexroof until the end of your next turn”.  
• {indent}SHREDDER: Create a 2/4 Gyro artifact creature token with flying, haste and vanishing 3 under chosen player's control.  
• {indent}STEALTH: Create a 0/1 Gyro artifact creature token with flying, shroud and {3} under chosen player's control. It has "Sacrifice this creature: You phase out until your next turn." *(Your permanents remain on the battlefield.)*”  
• {indent}PURGE: Create a 0/2 Gyro artifact creature token with flying under chosen player's control. It has “When this creature enters, you lose all counters.”  
• {indent}MEDIC: Create a 0/1 Gyro artifact creature token with flying under chosen player's control. It has “When this creature enters, you gain 5 life and draw a card.”
</RULES>
