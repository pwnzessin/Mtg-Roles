<TITLE>The Sneaky Pawn</TITLE>
<ROLE>Assassin</ROLE>
<SETCODE>PD3 c</SETCODE>
<RULES>
When The Sneaky Pawn is unveiled, exile up to three target instant and/or sorcery cards from graveyards.
For each card exiled this way, create a token creature with skulk and power equal to that card’s mana value and toughness 1. Then encode the exiled card onto that token. **(That token gains “Whenever this creature deals combat damage to a player, you may cast a copy of the encoded card without paying its mana cost.”)**
</RULES>
