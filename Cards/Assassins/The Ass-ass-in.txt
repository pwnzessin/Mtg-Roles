<TITLE>The Ass-ass-in</TITLE>
<ROLE>Assassin</ROLE>
<RULES>
When The Ass-Ass-in is unveiled you get an emblem with  
- “Creatures you control get \+0/+2.”  
- Until end of turn, whenever a creature attacks, choose an opponent at random. You may have it deal damage equal to its toughness to a creature that opponent controls.
</RULES>
