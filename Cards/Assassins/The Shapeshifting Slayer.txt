Unveil only if another non-Bandit, non-King role card is unveiled.  
When The Shapeshifting Slayer is unveiled, choose a creature on the battlefield, any graveyard, in exile or the command zone. Repeat this effect at the beginning of your next end step.  
Create a token that’s a copy of that creature and put it onto the battlefield under your control. It's a changeling in addition to its other types.
